Stage

LooperDisplay

Detects the first eight loopers in an Ableton Live set and shows their state as spinning rings in the browser — on the Mac, on a second monitor, or on any tablet on the same network. Tapping controls the loopers directly; optionally also via a KORG nanoKONTROL2 or Roland SPD-SX PRO.

Node.js Ableton Live Remote Script MIDI macOS · Apple Silicon

Desktop

Pendler-Monitor

Shows the next trains between Emsdetten and Münster live on the desktop, pops up automatically around commute times, warns about rain along the way and during lunch break — and can ping your phone if needed.
