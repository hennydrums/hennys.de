This site uses the system fonts of your device; the one additional font is embedded locally. There is no connection to Google Fonts servers or any other external font provider — no data is transmitted to third parties for this purpose.

Contacting me by email

The email links on this site open your own mail program. No data is transmitted to this website or its server in the process — the message goes directly from you to my inbox.

Linked external services

This site links to external services (including GitHub, Instagram, YouTube). These links only take you there after an active click; simply visiting this page transmits no data to those providers. Once you click through, that provider's own privacy policy applies.

StageCase: purchase, licence key and licence check

Purchase. StageCase is sold through Stripe (Stripe Managed Payments), which acts as the reseller (“Merchant of Record”). Payment details, billing address and invoices are processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). I receive from Stripe your name, email address, the product bought and the payment status, but no card details.

Licence key. After the purchase my licence service creates a licence key and stores it together with your name, email address, edition, purchase reference and the activated Macs. The licence service runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). The key is sent to you by email via Resend (Resend, Inc., USA; sending region EU). Both providers act as processors; transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU standard contractual clauses.

Licence check in the app. When you activate a licence, the app sends the licence key, the name of your Mac and a short hardware identifier to the licence service, to verify the licence and to list the activated Macs (at most two). From time to time the app repeats this check in the background. Apart from this and the update check (which only fetches a version number), the app sends no data; your sets, music and lyrics stay on your Mac.

Legal basis and storage. Performance of the licence contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Licence data is kept as long as the licence exists; purchase records as long as tax law requires (up to 10 years). You can ask for a Mac to be released or for your data at any time via the contact above.
